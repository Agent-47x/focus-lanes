# Playbook — Legal disputes

*Playbook for `domain: legal`.* How disputes and claims are run. You and Claude are not lawyers — your lawyer makes the legal calls.

**Rules** and **Checklists** are pushed into every project of this domain as `PLAYBOOK.md` by the daily build;
**Lessons** are the dated history behind them (the build appends here from `- [legal] …` lines under `## Promote`
in any project's `MEMORY.md`). When a lesson has become a rule, move it to **Rules** as one line.

> STARTER — one example rule and one checklist. Replace them with your own.

## Rules
- Every exchange with the other side is in writing and filed the same day.

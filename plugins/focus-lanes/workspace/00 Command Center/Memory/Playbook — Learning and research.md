# Playbook — Learning and research

*Playbook for `domain: learning`.* How study material, research archives and learning projects are handled.

**Rules** and **Checklists** are pushed into every project of this domain as `PLAYBOOK.md` by the daily build;
**Lessons** are the dated history behind them (the build appends here from `- [learning] …` lines under `## Promote`
in any project's `MEMORY.md`). When a lesson has become a rule, move it to **Rules** as one line.

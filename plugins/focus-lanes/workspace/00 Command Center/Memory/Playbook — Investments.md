# Playbook — Investments

*Playbook for `domain: investment`.* How offers, positions and counterparties are handled.

**Rules** and **Checklists** are pushed into every project of this domain as `PLAYBOOK.md` by the daily build;
**Lessons** are the dated history behind them (the build appends here from `- [investment] …` lines under `## Promote`
in any project's `MEMORY.md`). When a lesson has become a rule, move it to **Rules** as one line.

> STARTER — one example rule and one checklist. Replace them with your own.

## Rules
- Every unsolicited offer is vetted in writing before any document, signature or transfer.

## Checklists
**Vetting an offer**
    1. Who is the contracting entity, and is it licensed for this activity? · 2. Who pays, and is it the same entity? · 3. Where is the money held? · 4. What happens on exit or default? · 5. Decide in writing

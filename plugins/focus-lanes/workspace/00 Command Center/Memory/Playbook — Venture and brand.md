# Playbook — Venture and brand

*Playbook for `domain: venture`.* How ventures, offers and public presence are built.

**Lessons** are the dated history behind them (the build appends here from `- [venture] …` lines under `## Promote`
in any project's `MEMORY.md`). When a lesson has become a rule, move it to **Rules** as one line.
